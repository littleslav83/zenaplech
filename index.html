Zena Plech

Family Keepsakes and Family Fun

Zena Plech is my pen name, a play on my grandmother's maiden name Zdena Plechsmidova, who passed away in 2009. She loved family gatherings. She loved frogs and collected frog figurines. She loved to be the hostess, she loved to cook and to bake, and most importantly, she loved to laugh.
